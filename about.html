Our Story

Sweet Layers began in 2018 with a simple belief: everyone deserves a cake that's as unique as their celebration. What started as a passion project baking for friends and family has grown into a beloved local bakery known for creating stunning, delicious custom cakes.

Every cake we create is handcrafted with meticulous attention to detail. We source only the finest ingredients - from premium chocolates to fresh local fruits - ensuring that every slice tastes as beautiful as it looks.

We don't just bake cakes; we create edible works of art that become the centerpiece of your special moments.
